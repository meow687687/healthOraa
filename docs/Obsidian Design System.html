Design System · v1.0

Obsidian

A dark, glassy, data-first system for dashboards. Deep near-black canvases, one electric accent per screen, soft rounded surfaces and calm, readable numbers. Distilled from three references: a lime ride-earnings dashboard, an indigo e-commerce overview and a 3D logistics globe.

Principles

Four rules that every screen should obey.

1. One hero accent

Each screen leads with a single accent (lime or blue). Others only support.

2. Depth by glass

Layered surfaces with 1px light borders and faint gradients, never heavy shadows.

3. Numbers are heroes

Big, tight, bold figures. Labels stay small and muted.

4. Soft geometry

Large radii, pill controls, rounded bars and rings. No sharp corners.

Color

Click any swatch to copy its hex. Surfaces step up in lightness; accents are used sparingly.

Data visualization

Charts are quiet until something matters. Highlight one datum in the accent; everything else stays grey or translucent.

Acceptance
Rating
Smooth line + soft fill + ringed marker
Categorical bars, status palette
  • Max 1 accent colour per chart, status colours only for state.
  • Rings start at 12 o’clock, clockwise, with the value centred.
  • Gridlines at 6–8% white. Axis labels use caption style.

Usage & accessibility

Do
  • Use lime text only on dark surfaces; use #10140A on lime fills.
  • Keep card padding at 24px and gaps at 14–16px.
  • Pair every status colour with an icon or label.
  • Show a 4px focus ring in blue at 18% opacity.
Don’t
  • Mix lime and blue as equal accents on one screen.
  • Use pure #000 cards on a #000 page; step up surfaces.
  • Use body text below 13px or contrast below 4.5:1.
  • Animate more than one hero element at once.
